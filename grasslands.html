Grasslands National Park · Saskatchewan

Grasslands is booked. Here is a legal plan B.

Grasslands has two frontcountry campgrounds: Frenchman Valley in the West Block and Rock Creek in the East Block. Parks Canada warns that the Val Marie Visitor Centre and Rock Creek are 166 km apart, about a two-hour drive, so a plan B often means the other block, not the next campground down the road.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

With only one frontcountry campground per block, a sold-out night leaves few in-park options.

Parks Canada says water is available mid-May to 30 September only; campers in early May or October must bring all their water.
