Thousand Islands National Park · Ontario

The islands are booked. Here is a legal plan B.

Thousand Islands camping is on islands in the St. Lawrence, reached only by boat. Parks Canada lists 28 reservable and 32 first-come, first-served island campsites, plus 5 reservable oTENTiks on the mainland at Mallorytown Landing. Overnight stays are not permitted at Jones Creek or Landons Bay.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

All tent sites are on islands with no potable water, so campers need their own boat or a water taxi, and the mainland has only five oTENTiks.
