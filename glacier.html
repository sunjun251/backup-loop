Glacier National Park · British Columbia

Rogers Pass is busy. Here is a legal plan B.

Glacier National Park has two frontcountry campgrounds open on the Trans-Canada Highway at Rogers Pass. Parks Canada says 50% of Illecillewaet’s sites remain first-come, first-served. Loop Brook is reservation only. Mount Sir Donald frontcountry campground is closed until further notice.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

There are only two open frontcountry campgrounds in the park, and Parks Canada notes that very few sites take Class A motorhomes over 10 m (33 ft).

Booking tips

  • Parks Canada’s 2027 launch for Illecillewaet and Loop Brook: Wednesday, 13 January 2027 at 8 a.m. Pacific time.
  • If you are relying on an Illecillewaet first-come site, Parks Canada asks you to pick a site that fits your equipment and check in as directed on site.
