Yoho National Park · British Columbia

Yoho is booked. Here is a legal plan B.

Yoho has four frontcountry campgrounds. Parks Canada lists Hoodoo Creek as first-come, first-served only, and Monarch as first-come only in the spring and fall shoulder season. When those are full, the legal backups are campgrounds in neighbouring national parks and commercial campgrounds in Golden.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

Parks Canada’s Yoho camping page says demand is heaviest on long weekends and on all weekends in July and August, and that first-come sites fill up, so it tells campers to arrive early.

Lake O’Hara camping and the Lake O’Hara day-use bus have their own reservation and random-draw systems. They are not a same-day backup.

Booking tips

  • Parks Canada’s 2027 launch: Kicking Horse and Monarch open Tuesday, 19 January 2027 at 8 a.m. MT. Takakkaw Falls is listed with the mountain-park backcountry launch on Monday, 25 January 2027 at 8 a.m. MT. Lake O’Hara camping opens Thursday, 21 January 2027 at 8 a.m. MT.
  • Parks Canada lists a queue for this launch. Anyone on the waiting page in the 30 minutes before opening is randomly placed in line; see how the queue works.
  • If you are counting on Hoodoo Creek, arrive in the morning, near the 11 a.m. check-out, and keep a booked backup.
