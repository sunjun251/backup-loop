Torngat Mountains National Park · Newfoundland and Labrador

Torngat Mountains: no campground to sell out

Torngat Mountains National Park is not on Parks Canada’s reservation launch calendar for frontcountry camping. Parks Canada says the Torngat Mountains Base Camp, next to the park’s southern boundary, offers packaged trips with flights from Goose Bay to Saglek. Air or boat charters from Nain are another summer option. A plan B here means planning with the park office and licensed operators, not finding a backup campground.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only where the park allows and follow the park’s registration, orientation and safety rules. Parks Canada’s visitor information page explains what applies before you go.

Why it fills up

There is no roadside campground. The limiting factors are transport, weather and permits, not campsite inventory.

Booking tips

  • Parks Canada’s park contact: 1-709-922-1290.
  • Check thetorngats.com for base camp updates, as Parks Canada suggests.
