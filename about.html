About this site

An independent sample guide, not a park agency

Backup Loop is a sample directory that helps travellers find a legal place to camp when a popular Canadian national park campground is sold out. It is not Parks Canada, not a provincial or territorial park agency, and not affiliated with any campground it lists.

Not affiliated with Parks Canada or any provincial park agency

Backup Loop is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or approved by the Parks Canada Agency, His Majesty the King in right of Canada, the Government of Alberta, Alberta Forestry and Parks, Alberta Parks, any other provincial or territorial park agency, or any national park, provincial park, or private campground named on this site. Official marks, beaver logos, and park wordmarks are not used here and should not be inferred from our name or layout.

Always treat Parks Canada, the provincial or territorial park agency, and the campground operator as the authority if anything on this site disagrees with them.

What we publish

This example currently includes a home page, guides for Banff, Jasper and the other national parks listed on the home page, this about page, and a disclaimer. Each park page is organized the same way: reservable in-park campgrounds, first-come or overflow options as officially described, nearby provincial parks, then private alternatives, with sources at the bottom.

We summarize public facts and link to official pages. We do not copy copyrighted visitor guides or listing databases, and we do not invent reviews, prices, or unofficial “secret” camping spots. Where a price or procedure is unclear, we say to check the official site.

Affiliate and advertising disclosure

Some links on the park pages are Amazon.ca affiliate search links, labelled as advertisements. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you click one of those links and buy something, Backup Loop may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We do not claim any specific product, price, or rating.

Some other buttons on this site are labelled “Ad · Placeholder” and point to #affiliate-placeholder. They are not live booking links. If those placeholders are later replaced with referral links to services such as Hipcamp or Booking.com, Backup Loop may earn a commission if you book through them, at no extra cost to you.

Canada’s Competition Bureau states that marketers, including affiliates, must clearly and prominently disclose any material connection — such as a commission — on the page itself. A bio-only or footer-only disclosure is not enough. The U.S. Federal Trade Commission’s endorsement guides set a similar expectation where a U.S. audience is reached. That is why a disclosure appears in the visible notice at the top of every page, next to the relevant buttons, and again here.

Links to Parks Canada, provincial and territorial park agencies, and an operator’s own website are provided so you can book or confirm facts directly. Those official links are not affiliate links.

Accuracy and updates

Campground seasons, fees, fire bans, wildlife closures, and reservation rules change, sometimes with little notice. The research cited on the Banff and Jasper pages was checked on 19 November 2026; other park pages show their own check date (reported in the Asia/Manila time zone). It can already be out of date the next day. Verify every fact before you travel.

Safety and the law

Follow official park rules, fire bans, wildlife closures, and safety guidance. Camp only in designated, legal sites. This site is for general planning. It is not legal advice, trip advice, or a guarantee that a listed campground will be open, safe, or suitable for your vehicle or group.
