Kluane National Park and Reserve · Yukon

Kathleen Lake is full. Here is a legal plan B.

Kathleen Lake is Kluane’s highway-accessible campground: 38 sites, with a portion reservable through Parks Canada and the rest first-come. When it is full, Government of Yukon campgrounds in the Kluane region are the legal backup.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

One frontcountry campground serves the park, and only part of it can be reserved.

Yukon government campground

Pine Lake Campground

Yukon.ca: 62 sites at Alaska Highway km 1572, close to Haines Junction, with some reservable sites. Yukon warns it is often full on weekends. 2026 serviced season was 8 May–1 October.

Other Kluane-region campgrounds

Yukon lists Congdon Creek, Dezadeash Lake and others in the Kluane region on its campground status page. Check status before you drive.

Booking tips

  • Parks Canada’s 2027 launch for Kathleen Lake: Wednesday, 13 January 2027 at 8 a.m. Yukon time.
  • Yukon government campgrounds have their own booking site; Yukon.ca links to it from each campground page.
