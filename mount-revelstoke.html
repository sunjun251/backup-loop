Mount Revelstoke National Park · British Columbia

Snowforest is full. Here is a legal plan B.

Snowforest Campground is Mount Revelstoke’s frontcountry campground, and Parks Canada says the majority of its sites are reservable. Without a reservation, ask at the Campground Centre. When it is full, the closest legal backups are two BC Parks campgrounds near Revelstoke and Glacier National Park to the east.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

One frontcountry campground serves the park, so a full Snowforest leaves no other drive-in campground inside Mount Revelstoke.

Neighbouring national park

Glacier National Park

Parks Canada: two campgrounds 70 km east on the Trans-Canada Highway; about half of Illecillewaet’s sites are first-come.

Commercial campgrounds in Revelstoke

Booking tips

  • Parks Canada’s 2027 launch for Snowforest: Wednesday, 13 January 2027 at 8 a.m. Pacific time.
  • A national park pass is needed in addition to the campsite fee, per Parks Canada.
  • BC Parks campgrounds book separately at camping.bcparks.ca.
