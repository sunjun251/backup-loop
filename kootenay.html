Kootenay National Park · British Columbia

Kootenay is booked. Here is a legal plan B.

Parks Canada lists all Redstreak sites as reservable, and Marble Canyon and McLeod Meadows as reservation only. There is no first-come campground to fall back on inside Kootenay, so the plan B is a BC Parks campground near Radium Hot Springs or a first-come site in a neighbouring park.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

With every frontcountry site sold through the reservation system, a sold-out night in Kootenay means no walk-up options in the park.

Campgrounds inside the park

CampgroundHow to get a siteOfficial notes
RedstreakReservable (all sites)2.5 km above the village of Radium Hot Springs. Includes 10 oTENTiks. Cell service available.
McLeod MeadowsReservation onlyOn the Kootenay River near the Dog Lake Trail.
Marble CanyonReservation onlySub-alpine, northern part of the park. No cell coverage.

Parks Canada: check-in 2 p.m., check-out 11 a.m. Watch for cancellations on the Reservation Service.

Booking tips

  • Parks Canada’s 2027 launch for Redstreak, Marble Canyon and McLeod Meadows: Tuesday, 19 January 2027 at 8 a.m. MT.
  • Parks Canada lists a queue for this launch. Anyone on the waiting page in the 30 minutes before opening is randomly placed in line; see how the queue works.
  • BC Parks campgrounds have their own reservation system at camping.bcparks.ca.
