Elk Island National Park · Alberta

Astotin Lake is full. Here is a legal plan B.

Elk Island has one frontcountry campground, Astotin Lake, plus a group area and the hike-in Oster Lake backcountry site. Parks Canada says campgrounds close after the Thanksgiving long weekend and reopen for the May long weekend, and it names several nearby places to camp, including Miquelon Lake and Vermilion provincial parks.

Facts below were checked against the official pages linked on this page on 28 November 2026. Where we could not confirm a detail, we left it out or say “check official site”. Confirm dates, fees and booking rules before you travel.

Camp only in designated sites

Camp only in designated campgrounds and sites, with the camping permit and park pass Parks Canada requires. Do not sleep in roadside pullouts, parking lots, trailheads or day-use areas. If every legal site is full, drive to a legal backup.

Why it fills up

Parks Canada describes Astotin Lake as less than an hour east of Edmonton, so it draws weekend campers from the city, and there is no second frontcountry campground.

Booking tips

  • Parks Canada’s 2027 launch for Astotin Lake and Oster Lake: Wednesday, 20 January 2027 at 8 a.m. Alberta time.
  • Parks Canada lists a queue for this launch. Anyone on the waiting page in the 30 minutes before opening is randomly placed in line; see how the queue works.
  • Alberta Parks campgrounds book at albertaparks.ca/reservations.
